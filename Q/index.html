James Lee

01 — Who he actually is

Two identities, one ceiling

The name "James Lee" is doing double duty in this series, and getting the scaling right starts with not conflating them.

Diego Kang → "James Lee"

The original. Rose during the era when Kings and Circles carved up territory, then systematically dismantled that structure. Trained his legs specifically so his attacks couldn't be tracked, and challenged each of the Kings at least once — explicitly to measure himself against the ceiling.

→ This is the scaling subject

DG

The identity he built after the goal was accomplished. A celebrity, folded into Charles Choi's orbit and counted among his Ten Geniuses — which is the tell that his value was never purely physical.

→ Same person, later era

"James Lee" (2nd body)

The name got handed down. Daniel Park's second body operates under it. Different character, different feats, different tier — do not pool their showings.

→ Excluded from this file
02 — The argument

The scaling chain

Lookism has no numbers on the page. So you anchor to the one quantifiable showing the verse has, chain to it through direct in-story comparisons, and mark clearly where the chain stops being evidence and starts being inference.

1
Anchor · load-bearing

Goo Kim sets the speed floor

The verse's top band is pinned to Goo Kim. Pressure-testing against Younger Goo — forcing him to dual wield to keep up — is the feat VS Battles uses to put Goo at at least Supersonic. Everything above the mid-tier band inherits from here.

YieldsAt least Supersonic · Large Building level attack potency
2
Direct comparison

Gun Park sits level with Goo

Gun and Goo have gone at each other repeatedly and neither has produced a clean win. Stalemate across multiple encounters is about as strong as equality evidence gets in a feat-poor verse — so Gun takes the same band, and Gun's constant Ultra Instinct state is a quality modifier on top of it, not a tier bump.

YieldsGun Park ≈ Goo Kim — the God tier baseline
3
Direct comparison

Daniel's 2nd body brackets the God tier

Daniel Park fractured the arms of both Gun and Goo in separate confrontations. That's not "he scales near them" — that's damage output landing on established God tier durability. It fixes the top of the ladder as a tight cluster rather than a staircase.

YieldsThe God tier is a narrow band, not a spread
4
Cross-generation · inference

The Kings are the 1st Gen's ceiling

Taesoo Ma and Gongseob Ji weren't background monsters — they were the apex of the era James was measuring himself against, and both carry permanent damage from him: Ma's right eye, and Ji's leg loss. Costing the top of a generation body parts is a hard physical statement about output, and neither man is treated as beneath the modern top tiers.

YieldsJames Lee clears the 1st Gen ceiling outright
5
This site's read

Therefore: baseline at the God tier, above it with Path

Chain it: James beats the ceiling of his generation (4) → that ceiling isn't written as inferior to the modern apex → the modern apex is the Gun/Goo band (2, 3) anchored by Goo's speed feat (1). VS Battles lands him At least High 8-C, higher with Path, which is exactly this: baseline at the God tier, escalating past it in his upper states.

Where I'd argue harder than the wiki: the "higher with Path" clause is doing more work than the numbers show, because Path has never been given a bounded on-page ceiling. That's an upward-open modifier, not a fixed multiplier.

VerdictAt least High 8-C · Large Building level · at least Supersonic · upward-open with Path
03 — The receipts

Feat dossier

Each entry is framed for the panel that proves it. Drop your images in and the citations line up underneath.

Ended an era single-handed

The Circles collapsed because the Kings holding them up got beaten. Not a coalition, not attrition — one fighter clearing the board. Structural feats like this scale poorly on their own, but they establish that nobody in the generation had an answer.

Weight: high · sets the ceiling

Permanently mutilated the apex

Ma keeps that injury for the rest of the series. Gongseob Ji's right leg goes the same way. Permanent damage inflicted on generation-apex durability is the cleanest raw-output evidence in the file — it survives every retcon because the scars stay on the page.

Weight: high · attack potency

Trained past visual tracking

He built his legs specifically so opponents couldn't see the strike coming. Combat speed described as untrackable by fighters who are themselves elite is the qualitative version of the Goo anchor — it's why he inherits the verse's top speed band rather than sitting under it.

Weight: medium-high · speed

Ran the gauntlet on purpose

He sought out the Kings individually to gauge his own level. That's a stamina and consistency feat as much as a power one — repeated fights against the top of a generation without a recorded loss, treated in-story as calibration rather than struggle.

Weight: medium · stamina, consistency

Counted as a genius, not muscle

Charles Choi collects utility. Being placed among the Ten Geniuses and helping rebuild an empire is an intelligence rating with in-story backing — relevant because Lookism fights are repeatedly decided by read and adaptation, not just output.

Weight: medium · intelligence

Awakening and Path

Both wiki revisions treat these as escalators above his baseline: "higher with Awakening," then "higher with Path." Neither has a demonstrated hard ceiling on the page, which is the single largest source of variance in any James Lee matchup.

Weight: contested · upward-open

06 — How this was built

Method & honest limits

The rules I held myself to, including where the argument is weakest.

Anchor, then chain

One quantified feat carries the verse — Goo's speed showing. Everything else reaches the top band by chaining to it through in-story comparison, never by assertion.

Direct > transitive

"A beat B" outranks "A is like C who is like B." Gun/Goo stalemates and Daniel's arm breaks are direct; the 1st Gen bridge is not, and it's marked accordingly.

Confidence is labeled

Every link carries a tag. Hard is on-page. Soft is reasonable inference. Mine is my own read and you're allowed to reject it.

Limit: cross-generation gaps

The weakest link is step 4. Comparing 1st Gen fighters to the modern God tier relies on the series never writing the Kings as outclassed — an absence of evidence, not evidence. Kill that assumption and James drops a band.

Limit: Path has no ceiling

"Higher with Path" is unbounded on the page. That makes his upper end unfalsifiable, which is a weakness in an argument, not a strength. I've kept the baseline claim separate from it for exactly this reason.

Limit: the wiki moves

VS Battles Lookism tiers get revised regularly — the profile itself carries an original and an upgraded line. Treat every number here as a snapshot with a date, not a settled fact.

Adding your panels. Drop images into Q/panels/ using the filenames the empty frames show — feat-kings.jpg, feat-taesoo.jpg, feat-legs.jpg, feat-gauntlet.jpg, feat-genius.jpg, feat-path.jpg. They render automatically; anything missing keeps its labeled placeholder, so the page never breaks.
