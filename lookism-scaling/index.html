09 — Your panels

Panel manager

Every feat card is a drop target. Drag an image onto it, or click to browse — it saves to this browser and stays put across reloads. No files to commit, no filenames to match.

How it works

Images are stored locally in your own browser (localStorage), not uploaded anywhere and not committed to the repo. A personal reading copy stays personal — nothing you add here enters git history or a published page.

Export writes your panel set to a JSON file you can back up or move to another machine; Import reads one back in. To commit an image normally instead, drop it in panels/ and set that feat's file field in the data block near the bottom of this file — committed images load automatically and stay until something is dropped on top.

Browser storage caps out around 5MB, so resize large scans before adding many. The counter tracks where you stand.

10 — How this was built

Method & honest limits

The rules I held to, and the four places this is weakest.

Anchor, then chain

The verse has a handful of quantified showings — the calc'd lifting feats at mid tier, one speed anchor at the top. Everything else reaches its band by chaining to those through in-story comparison, never by assertion.

Direct beats transitive

"A fought B to a standstill" outranks "A is like C who is like B." Stalemates and landed damage are direct; generational bridges are not, and are marked.

Confidence is labeled

Hard is on-page. Soft is reasonable inference. Mine is my own read — reject it freely.

Limit: no chapter numbers

Feat cards carry era and arc tags, not chapter citations. I'd rather leave that field for you to fill from your own reading than print numbers I can't verify — a fabricated citation is worse than none, because it looks checkable and isn't.

Limit: the anchor's attribution is muddy

The Younger Goo dual-wield exchange is cited on more than one profile, and the wiki's wording differs on whose feat it is. The Supersonic band for the top tier holds either way, but the ambiguity is real.

Limit: keys and unbounded escalators

Half this cast has multiple "keys" — versions at different points in the story — and several have escalators with no stated ceiling (Path, Moonlight Style, Beast Mode, Ultra Instinct). Baselines are kept separate from them here, and any matchup involving one is uncertain by construction.
